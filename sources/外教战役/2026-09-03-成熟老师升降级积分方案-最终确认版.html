方案定位

机制已确认 Confirmed

Scheme Positioning

成熟老师只围绕一个主分数成长，推荐与治理不越权成为第二套考核。Senior teachers grow around one primary score; recommendation and governance do not become a second assessment system.

▍三条铁律 Three iron rules

铁律 1 · Rule 1

老师端唯一醒目的数字是「本周期总积分」，不展示第二个 0-100 分。The only prominent number shown to teachers is the "current cycle total points"; no second 0–100 score.

铁律 2 · Rule 2

升降级只依据完整周期五维总积分 + 必修前置，短期推荐状态不参与。Promotion/demotion depends only on full-cycle five-dimension total points + mandatory prerequisite; short-term recommendation status does not participate.

铁律 3 · Rule 3

高积分不能绕过治理：A2-A4 未解除时不得恢复正常推荐或接课。High points cannot bypass governance: while A2–A4 is unresolved, normal recommendation or class-taking cannot resume.

▍降级线与确认规则 Downgrade lines & confirmation

▍结算公式与判定 Settlement formula & decision

CycleTotalPoints = Σ(周期内五维正向积分 − 五维负向扣分) // five-dimension positive − negative in cycle
UpgradeEligible = 必修任务全部完成 // all mandatory tasks completed
DowngradeLine(L) = UpgradeLine(L − 2)

IF CycleTotalPoints ≥ UpgradeLine(L) AND UpgradeEligible
    → 次月 1 日升级 1 级 // promote 1 level on the 1st of next month
ELSE IF CycleTotalPoints < DowngradeLine(L) AND 满足降级确认 // AND confirmation met
    → 次月 1 日降级 1 级 // demote 1 level on the 1st of next month
ELSE
    → 保级，滚动刷新积分周期 // hold level, refresh rolling cycle

▍结算与清账规则 Settlement & clearance rules

次月 1 日生效 · Effective next month

升级/降级即时生效，旧等级积分清零，从新等级重新累计。Immediate effect; old points reset, restart from new level.

跨等级不串分 · No points across levels

窗口内出现等级变化则该窗口不参与判定，变级前积分不带入新等级。A window with a level change does not participate; pre-change points do not carry over.

同一事件只记一次 · Same event once

可追溯至课程/任务记录，治理动作不重复扣分。Traceable to class/task records; no double-deduction.

熔断流程 Circuit-breaker flow：T 事实发生（分钟级进队列）→ T+1 召回确认 → T+4 执行动作 → 整改观察 → 恢复回写。T event (minute-level queue) → T+1 recall → T+4 action → remediation → restore write-back.

执行优先级 Execution priority：A4 ＞ A3 ＞ A2 ＞ 短期推荐状态。A2-A4 立即改变推荐/预约/授课资格，但不直接改 Level、不重复扣分、不清零周期总积分。A2–A4 immediately change recommendation/booking/teaching eligibility, but do not change Level, do not double-deduct, do not reset cycle points.
