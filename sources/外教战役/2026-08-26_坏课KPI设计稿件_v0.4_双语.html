5学生反馈类：Leon 主张不纳入，定位为「探针 + 质检触发」Student Feedback: Leon's Position — Excluded, Positioned as "Probe + QC Trigger"

Leon 主张 · 已定：客户主观反馈不纳入坏课Leon's position · decided: customer subjective feedback is not a bad lesson
投诉、拉黑、差评——不进坏课。它是很好的探针：让我们对「客户对什么不满」保持持续洞察；它可以触发质检；但它不应该放入坏课。Complaints, blocking, bad reviews — not bad lessons. They are a great probe: ongoing insight into "what customers are dissatisfied with"; they can trigger QC; but they should not be inside the bad-lesson box.
三条理由：① 客户主观；② 不均匀（评价率仅 30–40%、样本偏）；③ 可被干预（恶意差评 / 刷分 / 曝光分配污染）Three reasons: ① subjective; ② uneven (only 30–40% evaluation rate, biased sample); ③ manipulable (malicious reviews / gaming / exposure-allocation pollution)

5.1 为什么主观反馈不能进坏课（P0 的必然推论）Why subjective feedback fails P0

  • 主观：口味、期望、心情差异大；同一行为不同客户结论不同——无法形成统一标准Subjective: tastes/expectations/moods differ; no uniform standard possible
  • 不均匀：评价率仅 30–40%；拉黑/差评受曝光分配影响（好老师前置会抬高其客户指标——H2 已标测量悖论）Uneven: only 30–40% rate; block/bad-review skewed by exposure allocation (better placement inflates customer metrics — H2 flags this measurement paradox)
  • 可被干预：恶意差评、老师刷分——一旦进 KPI 就有操纵动机Manipulable: malicious reviews, gaming — entering a KPI creates manipulation incentives

5.2 探针与质检触发的落地机制How the probe & QC trigger work

位子Role做法How
① 探针：持续洞察Probe: ongoing insight 投诉/拉黑/差评的主题聚类与趋势作为经营洞察看板——回答"客户最近对什么不满"，驱动治理方向与产品改进。坏课率复盘必须对照探针看（率降了但客户仍不满 → 说明口径没打到真问题）。Thematic clustering & trends of complaints/blocks/bad reviews as an insight dashboard — answers "what are customers unhappy about lately" and drives governance and product. Bad-lesson review MUST be read against the probe (rate down but customers still unhappy → the definition missed the real problem).
② 质检触发QC trigger 投诉/差评 → 触发质检/人工复核 → 核验成立且对应①②③类客观事件 → 该课次按对应类别正常计入分子（按课程 ID 去重）。即主观反馈是发现机制，不是分子本体。Complaint/bad review → trigger QC/human review → if confirmed and matches an ①②③ event → count the lesson in that category's numerator (dedup by lesson ID). Subjective feedback is a discovery mechanism, not the numerator itself.
③ 归 H2 客户尺To H2 customer ruler 与纪律/稳定/行为无关的体验反馈（口音、风格、级别错配）→ 归 H2 好老师客户尺（用户反馈维）与师生匹配。Non-discipline/stability/conduct feedback (accent, style, level mismatch) → goes to the H2 good-teacher customer ruler (user-feedback dimension) and matching.
📌 一句话总结In one sentence
坏课率管「客观的坏」；学生反馈管「客户的不满」——前者是 KPI，后者是探针。两者对照使用：坏课率下降而探针仍亮，说明有口径外的问题；探针触发质检后，把客观证据回填进坏课率。The bad-lesson rate governs "objective bad"; student feedback governs "customer dissatisfaction" — the former is the KPI, the latter the probe. Read them together: rate down but probe still lit = problems outside the definition; after QC is triggered, verified objective evidence flows back into the rate.
