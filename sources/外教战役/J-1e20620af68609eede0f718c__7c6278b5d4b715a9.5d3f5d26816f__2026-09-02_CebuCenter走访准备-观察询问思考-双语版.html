②观察什么（眼睛先于嘴巴：进每个中心先安静看 15 分钟）What to observe (eyes before mouth: spend the first 15 minutes in each center quietly watching)

现场运行Site operations
  • 座位利用率：数座位、数人头、数空位——早/中/晚三个时段各自坐满几成？空着的产能就是成本。Seat utilization: count seats, heads, empty spots — how full is each of the morning/midday/evening blocks? Idle capacity is pure cost.
  • 一天的真实节律：几点最挤、几点最空？错配是 Center 模式最大的隐性成本。The day's real rhythm: when is it most crowded, when most empty? Time-slot mismatch is the biggest hidden cost of the center model.
  • 上课画面：老师的状态是「紧绷应付」还是「从容投入」？隔几分钟看一间，比任何汇报都真。Lessons in progress: do teachers look strained-and-coping, or calm-and-engaged? Glance into a room every few minutes — truer than any briefing.
  • 异常怎么流动：断网/断电/设备故障时，谁在多长时间内怎么处理？（直接问最近一次事故即可）How incidents flow: when network/power/equipment fails, who handles it, how, and how fast? (Just ask about the most recent incident.)
老师Teachers
  • 来 Center 的真实原因：图设备？图氛围？图稳定课量？还是家里没法上课？——答案决定 Center 的价值主张。Why they actually came: for the equipment? the atmosphere? stable class volume? or because they can't teach at home? — The answer defines the center's value proposition.
  • 通勤与时间账：每天花多少时间在路上？和居家相比划算吗？Commute and time math: how much time per day on the road? Does it pay off versus teaching from home?
  • 流失信号：最近谁走了、为什么走、去哪了（有没有被竞对/Polly 挖）。Attrition signals: who left recently, why, and where did they go (poached by competitors / Polly?).
  • 毕业老师去向：升级 HBT/离开后，和中心还剩什么关系？（非零和问题的现场证据）Where graduates go: after upgrading to HBT or leaving, what relationship remains with the center? (Field evidence for the non-zero-sum question.)
基础设施Infrastructure
  • 电力与网络冗余：停电时 UPS/备用网络撑多久？频次多少？Power & network redundancy: during an outage, how long do UPS/backup links hold? How often do outages happen?
  • 设备：电脑新旧、耳机/摄像头状况、谁出钱维修；顺带核一眼电脑租赁试点的落地情况。Equipment: age of the computers, state of headsets/cameras, who pays for repairs; also verify on the spot how the laptop-rental pilot has landed.
  • 环境细节：隔音、空调、桌椅、饮水、休息区——老师每天 8 小时的真实体感。Environment details: soundproofing, aircon, desks and chairs, drinking water, break area — the teachers' real 8-hours-a-day experience.
数据与系统Data & systems
  • 中心负责人每天看什么数据：打开他实际用的页面/表格看一眼，别听他描述。What data the manager looks at daily: open the pages/sheets he actually uses and look — don't listen to descriptions.
  • 哪些事靠系统、哪些靠人盯：排课、请假、替补、考勤各自怎么流转？What runs on systems vs. on people watching: how do scheduling, leave requests, substitution and attendance actually flow?
  • 中心往上汇报什么：汇报什么 = 上面看不见什么，这往往是最大的信息缺口。What the center reports upward: what gets reported = what HQ cannot see — often the biggest information gap.

③问什么（三类对象 + 培训线，问到能改变判断为止）What to ask (three audiences + the training line; push until the answer could change a judgment)

给中心负责人 / 现场管理者For the center manager / on-site leader

  1. 如果预算不加，明天让你提升中心产出一个单位，你会做什么？（测真实瓶颈：座位？老师？课量？管理？）With no extra budget, if you had to raise this center's output by one unit starting tomorrow, what would you do? (Probes the real bottleneck: seats? teachers? class volume? management?)
  2. 这个中心一个月的完整成本账能算清吗？哪些成本是你最头疼的？（对照第二曲线假说的全成本问题）Can you produce a complete one-month cost account for this center? Which costs hurt most? (Feeds the full-cost question in the second-curve hypothesis.)
  3. 坐在这里的老师，和居家老师比，交付质量差多少？差的那部分，值不值这些成本？Versus work-from-home teachers, how big is the delivery-quality gap for teachers sitting here? And is that gap worth these costs?
  4. 最近一次严重事故（断网/集体迟到/设备坏）是怎么处理的？多久恢复？How was the most recent serious incident (outage / mass lateness / equipment failure) handled? How long to recover?
  5. 老师流失率多少？走的人去了哪？招人补位要多久？What is the teacher attrition rate? Where do leavers go? How long does it take to backfill?
  6. 你最希望总部给你的一件东西是什么？最不希望总部管你的是什么？（测「总部底座 + 区域自治」的边界该画在哪）What one thing do you most want from HQ? What do you most want HQ to stop controlling? (Probes where the "HQ backbone + local autonomy" line should be drawn.)

给老师（8:00–10:00 复盘 + 面谈，Jennifer 已安排）For teachers (8:00–10:00 lesson debrief + interviews, arranged by Jennifer)

  1. 你上次在家上课是什么时候？如果明天 Center 关门，你会回家上课还是离开？（这一问直接测 Center 的真实粘性）When did you last teach from home? If the center closed tomorrow, would you go back to home-teaching or leave? (One question that directly measures the center's real stickiness.)
  2. 在这上课，你的课量/收入和以前比怎么样？Teaching here, how are your class volume and income compared with before?
  3. 这里最让你难受的一件事是什么？（别问「满意吗」，人会客气）What is the single most painful thing about teaching here? (Don't ask "are you satisfied" — people are polite.)
  4. 你觉得自己什么时候会被 AI 取代？——听他怎么理解自己的价值。When do you think AI will replace you? — Listen to how they understand their own value.

给培训线（必答题 4 的展开）For the training line (expands Must-Answer Q4)

  1. 新人从入职到第一次独立上课，完整路径是几步？每一步谁负责、最多人卡在哪一关？From onboarding to a new teacher's first solo class, how many steps are there? Who owns each step, and where do most people get stuck?
  2. 培训是专职培训师还是老老师带？带一个新人吃掉带教老师多少时间（这笔机会成本算进成本账了吗）？Is training run by a dedicated trainer or by senior teachers? How much of a mentor's time does one new hire consume (is that opportunity cost in the cost account)?
  3. 过关标准是什么？没过关的人去了哪——淘汰、降级还是回炉？What is the pass standard? Where do those who fail go — cut, downgraded, or sent back for retraining?
  4. 上岗前检查清单长什么样、谁签字？抽查老师：你上周被检查过吗？（清单贴在墙上 ≠ 清单在用）What does the pre-class checklist look like, and who signs it? Spot-check a teacher: "Were you checked last week?" (A checklist on the wall ≠ a checklist in use.)

④想什么（判断框架：带着这些尺子去，答案现场自己会浮现）What to think about (judgment frames: bring these rulers, and the answers will surface on site)

尺子 1 ｜ 最小物理化原则Ruler 1 | The minimum-physical-footprint principle

真正的问题不是「Center 是否比居家稳定」（几乎必然是），而是：为达到客户可靠性，最少需要多少物理设施？沿途不断问自己：眼前看到的每一项投入，居家 + 数字化感知 + 区域替补能不能用更低成本替代？The real question is not "is a center more stable than home teaching" (it almost certainly is), but: what is the minimum physical infrastructure needed to achieve customer-facing reliability? Keep asking along the way: for every investment you see, could home-based teaching + digital sensing + regional backup deliver it at lower cost?

尺子 2 ｜ Center 的四种可能身份Ruler 2 | Four possible identities of a center

这个中心到底在扮演哪个角色？核心可靠性节点 / 区域供给锚点（少量锚点 + 居家网络 + AI 调度）/ 经营卫生基础设施 / 阶段性学习工具。Cebu 三个中心业态各异（招生前端 / 新人放大器 / 新策略），很可能各自是不同身份——这本身就是最重要的发现。Which role is this center actually playing? Core reliability node / regional supply anchor (a few anchors + home network + AI dispatching) / operational-hygiene infrastructure / a transitional learning tool. The three Cebu centers have different shapes (recruiting front-end / new-teacher amplifier / new strategy) — they may each be a different identity, and that itself may be the most important finding.

尺子 3 ｜ 增量 vs 全成本Ruler 3 | Incremental value vs. full cost

验收标准不是「老师更准时、管理更清楚」，而是：相对于最佳居家方案，可归因的客户与经营增量，能不能覆盖场地、设备、网络、人员、空置产能的全部成本？现场能拿到的最好证据：中心老师 vs 居家老师的坏课率/出勤率/续费贡献对比。The bar is not "teachers are more punctual, management is cleaner" but: relative to the best home-based alternative, can the attributable customer and business increment cover the full cost of premises, equipment, network, staffing and idle capacity? The best evidence available on site: a center-vs-home comparison of bad-class rate, attendance and renewal contribution.

尺子 4 ｜ 非零和问题Ruler 4 | The non-zero-sum question

Quezon 已出现 CO 与毕业老师的竞争压力信号（尚待独立证据核实）。现场顺带验证：老师从中心「毕业」后，与中心是价值归零，还是能持续创造价值？CO 管理上最缺的那一项能力是什么？——这决定升级规则和本地 MVP 怎么设计。Quezon has shown early signals of graduated teachers competing with CO (independent verification still pending). Verify in passing on site: after a teacher "graduates" from the center, does the relationship go to zero value, or keep creating value? What is the one capability CO management most lacks? — This decides how the upgrade rules and the local MVP should be designed.
