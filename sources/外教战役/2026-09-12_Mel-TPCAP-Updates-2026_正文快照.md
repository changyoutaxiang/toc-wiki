### **TPCAP AUG EOM Performance Summary**

**SHARE**

##### **The overall** **Lesson Share** **reached 37.57% as of Aug 2026 EOM, showing an increase from 36.48% in July.**

### August 2026 EOM 

TPCAP ended August with strong booking and shouldering growth compared with July:
- Overall booked lessons reached **3,025,180**, up by **48,208 (\+1.62%)**. 
- TPCAP booked lessons increased to **1,172,867**, up by **45,209 (\+4.01%)**. 
- TPCAP RL booked lessons grew to **1,136,532**, an increase of **50,493 (\+4.65%)**. 
- Overall TPCAP shouldering improved from **36.48% to 37.57% (\+1.09 percentage points)**. 
- OVS shouldering increased to **41.14% (\+0.99 percentage points)**. 
- DOM shouldering improved to **33.99% (\+1.20 percentage points)**. 
- Overall utilization declined slightly from **78.10% to 76.02%**. 
- NT utilization improved from **52.46% to 54.78%**, while LN utilization decreased slightly from **54.53% to 53.70%**. 

Overall, August showed positive momentum in booking volume and lesson share across both OVS and DOM. The main focus moving forward should be improving overall utilization and addressing the slight decline in LN utilization while sustaining the growth in bookings and shouldering rate.

#### **1.5 TBT Performance vs CBT and HBT**

#####  **YTD Performance Analysis**

TBT is the strongest contributor relative to its size. Although it represents only **19.67% of total headcount**, it delivers **34.62% of lesson share**, showing that TBT teachers generate significantly more lessons than their workforce share.

Key strengths:
- Highest average active days: **24.05 days**
- Highest average open slots: **14.41**
- Highest average fixed slots: **58.01**
- Strong overall utilization: **74.13%**, above HBT’s **67.56%**
- Best attendance performance:
    - TAR: **1.98%**
    - Late rate: **0.17%**
    - Early rate: **0.06%**
    - Missed rate: **0.26%**
- Highest average earnings: **₱16,451.11**
- Highest student reach:
    - **99.58** unique students
    - **7.69** regular students
    - **5.37** My Students

##### **Key concern**

TBT’s **GE rate is 20.31%**, lower than both HBT at **21.66%** and CBT at **23.33%**. This suggests that while TBT teachers are highly productive and available, lesson quality and student experience still need improvement to generate more positive evaluations.

##### **Management takeaway**

TBT is delivering strong operational productivity, utilization, attendance, earnings, and student reach. Its lesson contribution is approximately **1.76 times its headcount share**. The next priority should be improving the GE rate through stronger coaching, class-quality monitoring, and student-experience initiatives while maintaining current availability and attendance performance.
